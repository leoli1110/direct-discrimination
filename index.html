Coordination check

Time-current selectivity limit is the lowest current where the downstream device's latest clearing time reaches the upstream device's earliest operating time. Relay grading uses the CTI from ECalPro's IEC 60255 methodology (0.4 s electromechanical, 0.3 s numerical). When both devices are in their instantaneous region, energy-based selectivity needs the manufacturer's selectivity tables.
